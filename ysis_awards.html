y-SIS Awards

The Best Young Contribution and Best PhD Student Contribution awards are annual honors promoted by y-SIS to recognize excellence, innovation, and scientific talent among early-career researchers in statistics.

The Best Young Contribution award, proudly sponsored by the Italian Statistical Society, is open to all young y-SIS members who author (or co-author) and present an accepted paper at the conference. The recipient is selected by a dedicated scientific committee based on research quality and impact. The Best PhD Student Contribution award is warmly sponsored by Tstat S.r.l. and is reserved for current PhD students and recent doctoral graduates. The winner is selected directly by the y-SIS board.

Explore the past recipients and honorable mentions below to view the award-winning papers and contributions from past years!
